# Brightpath Analytics — Candidate Communication Policy (v1)

Fictional company and policy, written for this prototype. This is the ONLY source of company facts the
drafting tool may use.

## General
- Sign every reply as "Brightpath Recruiting Team".
- Never share hiring decisions, interview feedback, rankings, or information about other candidates by message.
  Decisions and feedback come from the assigned recruiter directly.
- The drafting tool cannot see application records, interview calendars, or offer status. When an answer depends
  on them, say that the recruiter will check and follow up within 2 business days.

## Application status
- We aim to review every application within 10 business days of submission.
- Every application submitted through the careers portal receives an automatic confirmation email; if it did not
  arrive, the recruiter will check that the application was received.
- A candidate may move an existing application to a different open role; the recruiter arranges this, so a second
  application is not needed.

## Interviews
- Interview times are set by the recruiter, who sends a calendar invite containing the Google Meet link.
- A reply must never propose or confirm a specific date or time; the recruiter confirms times by calendar invite.
- Candidates may reschedule once with at least 24 hours' notice. Requests with less notice (for example emergencies)
  are reviewed case by case by the recruiter.
- Process for most roles: recruiter screen (30 minutes), skills interview (60 minutes), final panel (60–90 minutes).
- Dress code for onsite interviews is business casual.

## Documents
- CVs and portfolios can be uploaded on the careers portal (careers.brightpath.example) or sent as a PDF attachment
  in reply to this inbox.
- If the portal upload fails, use a PDF under 5 MB, or attach the file to a reply instead.
- Candidates attending a final-round onsite interview bring a government-issued photo ID.
- Transcripts and references are requested only at offer stage.
- Never ask for or accept national ID numbers, bank details, or passwords by email. If a candidate sends them, ask
  them not to send such details by email, say the message will be deleted from the inbox, and explain that the
  secure onboarding portal is used after an offer.

## Compensation and offers
- Do not state salary figures or ranges, and do not negotiate by message. The recruiter will arrange a call to
  discuss compensation.
- Offer letters are emailed by the recruiter after approval.

## Withdrawals and data
- Confirm the withdrawal, thank the candidate, and say they are welcome to apply again in future.
- Candidate data is kept for 12 months after the process ends. Candidates can ask for deletion at any time; deletion
  is completed within 30 days.

## Work arrangements and benefits
- Most roles are hybrid: 3 days per week in the Lahore or Karachi office. The recruiter confirms the arrangement for
  a specific role.
- Brightpath does not currently sponsor work visas.
- Open roles are listed on the careers portal.
- Benefits: health insurance for the employee and immediate family, 20 days of paid annual leave, and an annual
  learning budget.

## Accommodations
- Brightpath provides reasonable interview adjustments (for example captions, written components, extra time,
  flexible scheduling).
- Requests are passed confidentially to the People team, who will contact the candidate. Do not ask for medical
  details.

## Complaints
- Concerns about interview conduct are taken seriously and escalated to the Head of Talent, who will contact the
  candidate within 5 business days.
- Acknowledge and thank the candidate. Do not argue, speculate, or admit fault on behalf of individuals.

## Messages that are not from candidates
- Sales pitches and unrelated messages: draft a one-line polite note that this inbox handles candidate enquiries only.
